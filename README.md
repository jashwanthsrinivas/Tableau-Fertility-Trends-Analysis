Tableau Fertility Trends Analysis
Project Overview
This Tableau project explores fertility trends across selected Asian countries and examines how fertility relates to key socioeconomic and demographic factors.

The analysis combines multiple datasets using Country and Year to create a data-driven story examining potential relationships between fertility and education, marriage age, female labor participation, and access to family planning.

Business & Analytical Questions
The project explores questions such as:

How have fertility rates changed across selected Asian countries?
How does women's age at first marriage relate to fertility?
What relationship exists between female youth literacy and fertility?
How does government education spending relate to fertility?
How does female labor-force participation compare with fertility?
How might unmet contraception needs relate to fertility?
Tableau Analysis
The workbook includes several analytical views:

Fertility Rate vs Age at Marriage
Compares fertility rates with the mean age of women at first marriage.

Fertility Rate vs Female Youth Literacy
Examines fertility alongside female youth literacy across selected Asian countries.

Fertility Rate vs Government Education Spending
Compares fertility with public education spending as a percentage of GDP.

Fertility vs Female Labor Participation
Explores fertility alongside female labor-force participation.

Fertility vs Unmet Contraception Needs
Examines fertility together with unmet contraception needs among married women of reproductive age.

Fertility Trends: 2000 vs 2020
Uses a slope chart to compare fertility rates between 2000 and 2020 across selected countries.
